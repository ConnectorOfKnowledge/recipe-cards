Homemade
Vegan Butter

Seven things, a whisk, and an hour in the fridge. It spreads like the real thing and bakes like it too. The turmeric is only for color, so leave it out and nobody will know the difference.

The Move

Refined coconut oil, always

Refined coconut oil is flavorless, and that's the whole trick. Virgin or unrefined will taste like a coconut macaroon. The curdled soy milk is your dairy-free buttermilk; it's what makes this read as butter instead of just hardened oil.

02

Method

4 steps · ~1 hr 15 min total
  1. 1

    Make the buttermilk

    Stir the apple cider vinegar into the soy milk and let it sit about 5 minutes, until it curdles and thickens slightly.

  2. 2

    Melt the coconut oil

    Gently melt the refined coconut oil until it's just warm and fully liquid. Warm, not hot.

  3. 3

    Blend it smooth

    Add the melted coconut oil, neutral oil, coconut cream, salt, and the optional pinch of turmeric to the curdled milk. Whisk hard for under 5 minutes until it's smooth and pale, or run it through a blender or food processor.

    Pro tip A blender gives the silkiest, best-emulsified result. If you whisk by hand, keep going until there isn't a single streak of oil left.
  4. 4

    Chill to set

    Pour into a glass container or butter molds. Refrigerate 1 to 2 hours, until firm.

    For baking Soften it at room temperature for about 30 minutes first. It works in pie crust, cookies, and frosting.

Spread it. Bake with it.

It keeps 2 to 3 weeks in the fridge and freezes well, so make a double batch. Soften it 30 minutes on the counter before baking and it behaves like dairy butter.
